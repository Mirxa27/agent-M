*   **Template Studio:** Don't start from scratch. Design, customize, and deploy your own AI workflows or modify our extensive library to perfectly fit your business processes.
*   **130+ Pre-built AI Workflows:** Hit the ground running with a vast selection of ready-to-use templates for common tasks across industries. From client onboarding to content creation, we've got you covered.
*   **Multilingual Localization:** Effortlessly manage communications and documents in multiple languages. Perfect for global teams and international clientele.
*   **Financial Suite:** Streamline your financial paperwork with tools for creating and managing invoices, quotes, and other financial documents.
*   **Documents:** A dedicated space for your writing, notes, and project documentation. Create, edit, and organize all your essential texts in one place.
*   **Capture:** Instantly jot down ideas, inspirations, and to-dos. Link your captures to specific documents or projects for seamless context.
*   **Chat (with kAI Flows):** Engage with our pre-made kAI (Knowledge-driven AI) flows for guided task completion. Reference your documents, external sources, or even paste a YouTube video link to work with its transcript!

*   **Elements:** Structure and organize content within your documents effortlessly. Simply type "/" to access a menu of formatting options, embeds, and content blocks.
